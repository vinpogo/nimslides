# nimslides

Markdown slides in the terminal.

Press **→** for the next slide.

---

## Slides

Separate slides with a line of `---` that has a blank line above and below.

- `→` `l` `n` `space`: next slide
- `←` `h` `p` `backspace`: previous slide
- `↑` `↓` `j` `k`: scroll a tall slide

---

## Still markdown

A `---` right under text is a heading underline, and `***` draws a rule:

***

```markdown
A code block can contain

---

without splitting the slide.
```
