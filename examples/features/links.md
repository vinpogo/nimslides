Inline links show their target after the text: read the
[Nim manual](https://nim-lang.org/docs/manual.html "The manual")
or the [tutorial](<https://nim-lang.org/docs/tut1.html>).

Reference links keep long URLs out of the prose: the [standard
library][stdlib], a [collapsed one][] and a bare [shortcut].

[stdlib]: https://nim-lang.org/docs/lib.html
[collapsed one]: https://nimble.directory
[Shortcut]: https://forum.nim-lang.org "Forum"

Autolinks are shown as they are: <https://github.com/nim-lang/Nim>
and <hello@example.com>. So is a link whose text is its URL:
[https://nim-lang.org](https://nim-lang.org).

Labels without a definition stay literal: [not a link] and [this][nope].

A link inside link text is just text: [outer [inner](/x) text](/outer).
