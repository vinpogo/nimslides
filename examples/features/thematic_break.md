Three stars make a break:

***

So do three dashes, with a blank line above and text right below
(between two blank lines, `---` separates slides instead):

---
And three underscores:

___

Spaces between the marks are fine, and there can be more than three:

- - -

 **  *  ** * ** * **

-------------------------------------
A break interrupts a paragraph without a blank line
* * *
and the text continues as a new paragraph.

These are not breaks: two marks, the wrong character, or text after the marks.
