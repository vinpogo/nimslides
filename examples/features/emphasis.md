# Emphasis

Text can be *italic* or _italic_, **bold** or __bold__,
and ***bold italic*** or ___bold italic___.

They nest: **bold with *italic* inside** and *italic with **bold** inside*.

Stars work inside words: un*frigging*believable, and **foo**bar.
Underscores do not: snake_case_word stays as it is.

Lone stars stay literal: 2 * 3 * 4, and so does \*escaped\* text.
