Two spaces at the end of a line  
start a new line here,\
and a backslash does the same.

Many spaces work too:       
    and the next line's indent is ignored.

Spaces  inside   a line stay as they are, and a single space 
before a line ending is just a soft break.

Long lines still wrap normally around a hard break, like this one here  
and continue after it.

A break at the very end of a paragraph does nothing, and a final
backslash stays literal\
