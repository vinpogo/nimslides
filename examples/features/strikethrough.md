# Strikethrough

Two tildes ~~strike text out~~, and so does ~one~.

It mixes with other styles: ~~struck *and italic*~~, **bold ~~and struck~~**.

Inside words works too: un~~believ~~able.

Runs must match, so ~~two and one~ stays as it is.

Three tildes never strike: ~~~like this~~~.

Escaped tildes are literal: \~\~not struck\~\~.
